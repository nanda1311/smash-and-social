About Me

Mason Carter brings over 12 years of professional pickleball coaching experience to our academy. As a certified high-performance instructor, Mason specializes in helping players of all skill levels refine their technique, master court positioning, and develop winning strategies.

Mason’s personalized and encouraging teaching style ensures that each player receives tailored guidance designed to strengthen their footwork, paddle control, shot accuracy, and overall game IQ. His deep knowledge of pickleball mechanics — from spin generation to split-step timing — has helped countless players elevate their performance.

When he’s not coaching on the court, Mason enjoys participating in regional tournaments, testing new paddles, and traveling to experience emerging pickleball communities around the world.
