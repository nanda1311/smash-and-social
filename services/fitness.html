Fitness Studio

Strength, Redefined.

Whether you're chasing serious transformation or just want to move, sweat, and unwind , our gym has a track built for you.

01 / ROUTINE (BODYBUILDING)

Routine (Bodybuilding)

A serious, structured fitness offering for members focused on building strength, muscle, and long-term physical transformation.

  • Fully-equipped strength zone , free weights, machines, benches, racks
  • Personalized split routines and progressive overload tracking
  • Certified personal trainers for one-on-one or small group coaching
  • Nutrition and supplement guidance tied in with the cafe's healthy meals
  • Body composition scans, monthly transformation tracking, and PR boards
02 / HOBBY OR STRESS BUSTER

Hobby or Stress Buster (Corporate & Students)

A casual, low-pressure fitness offering for people who want to stay active and relieve stress , not necessarily chase aesthetic or performance goals.

  • Drop-in / flexible gym access with no rigid routine required
  • Group fitness classes , Zumba, HIIT, yoga, functional training
  • Short 30-45 min sessions and stress-relief focused classes
  • Corporate wellness packages and student-friendly off-peak pricing
  • Music-driven class vibe with "workout + healthy meal" combo tie-ins
