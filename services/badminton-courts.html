Professional Badminton Courts

Everything You Need
Under One Roof

From a casual game with friends to competitive tournaments, structured coaching to community play for seniors , explore all six of our badminton offerings.

01 / COURT BOOKING

Court Booking

On-demand and advance booking of premium badminton courts, designed for players who just want to walk in and play.

  • Hourly/slot-based booking via app, website, or walk-in
  • Multiple courts with professional-grade flooring and lighting
  • Peak and off-peak pricing, plus recurring weekly/monthly slots
  • Single, doubles, or group session bookings
  • Shower, changing room and hydration station access
02 / COACHING

Coaching

Structured, personalized training programs led by certified badminton coaches, for players at every stage of their journey.

  • Beginner to advanced programs (grip, footwork, smashes, strategy)
  • One-on-one private coaching and small group sessions
  • Age-specific kids' coaching batches
  • Video analysis of gameplay for technique correction
  • Flexible monthly, quarterly, or per-session packages
03 / SUMMER CAMPS

Summer Camps

A seasonal, immersive program combining skill-building with fun, social activities for kids and teens during school breaks.

  • Multi-day/week-long, age-grouped batches (6-10, 11-15)
  • Daily skill sessions, fun games, and mini-matches
  • Camp-exclusive merchandise and end-of-camp mini-tournament
  • Trained coordinators, coaches, and snack breaks included
  • Optional parent showcase day to watch a final session
04 / TOURNAMENTS & CHAMPIONSHIPS

Tournaments & Championships

Competitive events hosted regularly at Smashh & Social, open to different skill levels and age groups.

  • Singles and doubles formats across beginner, intermediate, and open/pro divisions
  • Age-group championships for juniors, adults, and seniors
  • Professional umpiring with live scoreboards and leaderboard tracking
  • Medals, trophies, and cash prizes based on tournament tier
  • Registration support for team formation, seeding, and byes
05 / CORPORATE HOSTING

Corporate Hosting

End-to-end event hosting for companies looking to organize badminton-based team activities, tournaments, or client engagement events.

  • Full court booking for private corporate use
  • Custom mini-tournament formats with full event coordination
  • Branding opportunities and catering add-ons via the cafe
  • Team-building games alongside badminton matches
  • Optional photography/highlight reel and participant mementos
06 / ELDERLY PEOPLE'S COMMUNITY

Elderly People's Community

A dedicated, low-pressure program designed to keep seniors physically active and socially connected through badminton.

  • Fixed weekly/bi-weekly sessions exclusively for seniors
  • Guided warm-ups and modified, doubles-focused recreational play
  • Trained facilitator present for safety and pacing
  • Social mixer time at the cafe after every session
  • Accessible, health-conscious environment with rest breaks
