Group Classes

Elevate Your Pickleball Skills With
Professional Coaching

Group classes are a fun, engaging, and cost-effective way to improve your pickleball skills while connecting with other players. These sessions bring together individuals of similar skill levels for structured training led by an experienced coach. Whether you’re a beginner learning the basics or an intermediate player refining strategy and footwork, group classes offer a balanced mix of instruction, practice, and friendly competition.

Exclusive pickleball membership & premium benefits

During each session, coaches break down essential techniques such as grip, paddle control, dinking, volleys, and proper footwork. Players also work on game-specific skills like serve and return consistency, positioning, and communication—crucial elements for doubles play. With drills tailored to the group’s ability, everyone gets equal attention and opportunities to practice in real-time situations.
